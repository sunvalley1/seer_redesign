// Resolution

Resolved by an open court.

Every Seer market resolves through Kleros — an on-chain court of independent jurors, staked and randomly drawn to arbitrate disputes. No centralized referee.

Kleros · Onchain justice
01

Answer proposed

Anyone can bond and submit the market's outcome once the reporting window opens.

02

Challenge window

If the proposed answer is disputed, the case escalates to Kleros with evidence attached.

03

Jurors decide

A randomly drawn panel of Kleros jurors reviews evidence and votes on the correct outcome.

04

Market settles

The ruling is written on-chain and outcome tokens settle at their true value.
